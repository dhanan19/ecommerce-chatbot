## Warranty and Support Policy

This document describes warranty coverage and how customers can reach ShopBot support.
Use it for questions about warranty length, replacements, and support hours — not for a specific order status.

### 1. Standard warranty

- Most electronics (mice, keyboards, headphones, chargers) include a **12-month limited warranty** from the delivery date.
- The warranty covers manufacturing defects in materials and workmanship under normal use.
- It does **not** cover accidental damage, liquid damage, lost accessories, or wear from normal use (for example, worn keycaps).

### 2. Warranty vs returns

- The **30-day return window** is for change-of-mind and standard returns (see returns_policy.txt).
- After 30 days, a defective item may still be eligible for **warranty repair or replacement** if it is within 12 months of delivery.
- Warranty claims still require a support ticket. ShopBot submits the request for **human admin review**; it is not auto-approved.

### 3. Replacements

- If a warranty claim is approved, we send a replacement of the same model when stock is available.
- If the model is discontinued, we offer a comparable replacement or store credit for the original paid amount.
- Customers should keep the original packaging when possible for warranty returns.

### 4. Support hours and channels

- ShopBot chat is available **24/7** for order status, policy questions, and submitting cancel/return requests.
- Human admin review of cancellations, returns, and warranty tickets is typically **Monday–Friday, 9:00–18:00** local store time.
- Email notifications (or simulated notifications in the lab environment) are written to `email_logs` whenever a request is decided.

### 5. What ShopBot can and cannot do

- ShopBot **can** look up this customer's orders, payments, tickets, and policy answers.
- ShopBot **cannot** cancel an order or approve a return without admin review.
- ShopBot will not share another customer's orders, tickets, or email history.
